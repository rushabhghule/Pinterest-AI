# Confusion Prevention

A quick check to run before every classification. The rules live in `CLASSIFICATION_RULES.md`. This file only lists mistakes that have happened before and how to avoid them.

Version: **v2.1 (30 Oct 2026)**

---

## Before you classify

1. Name the primary subject in three words or fewer. Ask "what am I photographing?", not "where is it?"
2. Walk the decision order in `CLASSIFICATION_RULES.md` from Step 1. Stop at the first "yes".
3. Check that the destination appears in `BOARD.md`. Never invent one.
4. If two steps feel equally true, use the earlier step and set confidence to Medium.

## Known traps

| Trap | Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|---|
| Kitten | Street Dog Photography | Cat Photography | Rule 1: species decides, not age |
| Cat held by a person | Randoms (portrait) | Cat Photography | Rule 1: animals win |
| Dog asleep outside a shop | Street Dog Photography | Street & Architecture Photography | Rule 1: a small animal in a wide scene is not the subject |
| Motorcycle in a parking garage | Street & Architecture Photography | Bike & Car Photography | Rule 3 |
| Bus at night | Night Photography | Bike & Car Photography | Step 2 comes before Step 3 |
| Bonfire after dark | Randoms | Night Photography | Rule 4: darkness is the look |
| Sunset with light still in the sky | Night Photography | Sky, Cloud & Sunset Photography | Rule 4 |
| Temple building | Idols and Statues | Street & Architecture Photography | Rule 6 |
| Deity statue inside a temple | Street & Architecture Photography | Idols and Statues | Rule 6: the idol is the subject |
| Fruiting tree | Flower Photography | Nature Photography | Rule 5: only blooms are flowers |
| Garlic pile, cracked earth, brick close-up | Nature or Flower Photography | Texture Photography | Rule 10 |
| Whole brick building | Texture Photography | Street & Architecture Photography | Rule 10: distance makes it architecture |
| Swimming pool or sea | Nature Photography | Water | Rule 9 (changed in v2.1) |
| Single object on a plain background | Randoms | Symmetry and Isolation | Rule 11 |
| Phone, watch or TV screen | Classified by what it shows | Randoms | Rule 8 |
| Close portrait of a friend | Street & Architecture Photography | Randoms | Rule 7 |
| Owner's favourite shot | fav bangers | Its normal destination | fav bangers is manual only |

## Do not

- Guess the photographer's intent. Classify only what is visible.
- Let the setting decide when a clear subject is present.
- Assign two destinations to one image.
- Update the rules yourself. Flag the case for the owner instead (see `WORKFLOW_PROMPT.md`).
