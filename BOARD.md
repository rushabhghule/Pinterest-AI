## Destinations

The AI may only use the destinations in this table. Never invent a board or section.

### Boards

| Board | What goes here |
|---|---|
| Street Dog Photography | Every dog: street dogs, known dogs, puppies |
| Cat Photography | Every cat: adult cats, kittens, orange cats |
| Bike & Car Photography | Motorcycles, cars, bicycles, buses, trucks as the subject |
| Night Photography | Photos where darkness or night is the defining look: city lights, bonfires, moonlit skies, streets after dark |
| Sky, Cloud & Sunset Photography | Dominant clouds, daytime skies, sunsets, golden hour |
| Flower Photography | Flowering blooms only |
| Texture Photography | Close-up surfaces: stone, brick, bark, soil, walls, piles of produce |
| Nature Photography | Trees, plants, leaves, fruits, crops, landscapes |
| Street & Architecture Photography | Buildings, temples as buildings, roads and paths, street scenes, graffiti |
| Idols and Statues | Idols, deity sculptures, statues, clay figurines |
